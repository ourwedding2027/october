Guest travel guide

Traveling to Cookstown

Northern Ireland

Belfast International Airport (BFS)

Best for most direct arrivals. Around 45 minutes by car to Glenavon Hotel.

Advice: Book a shared taxi in advance for easier weekend transfers.

Republic of Ireland

Dublin Airport (DUB)

Ideal for wider flight options. Journey time is about 2.5 hours by car.

Advice: Consider rental car or pre-booked coach for smooth border crossing.

England

Belfast City Airport (BHD)

Frequent short flights from major UK cities, then around 1 hour road transfer.

Advice: Friday afternoon flights fill quickly, so reserve early.

Hungary

Belfast International via London or Dublin

Most routes connect through London airports or Dublin, then transfer to Cookstown.

Advice: Choose flights with at least 2 hours layover for comfortable connections.
